Voxshield

AI-Powered Real-Time Voice Cloning Detection

AI voice cloning lets attackers convincingly impersonate the people we trust — executives, officials, family — over ordinary phone calls, driving financial fraud and social engineering. Caller ID and conventional voice recognition no longer hold. Voxshield is built to catch synthetic and cloned voices in real time, before the conversation can be weaponized.
